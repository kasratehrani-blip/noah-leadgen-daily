# Voice reference — HOW TO USE

Copy this file to `past-emails.md` in the same folder, then replace the examples below
with 5 to 10 real emails you (or Loek) actually sent that landed well. The drafter reads
these to copy the rhythm, warmth, and phrasing. The real file is gitignored.

The point is not the content, it is the voice. Notice in the examples: short, first name,
one dated reason up top, one small ask, no em-dashes, no corporate filler.

---

## Example 1

Hi [First name],

Saw [company] just [dated signal, e.g. picked up an EMI licence in the Netherlands].
Congrats. The reason I am reaching out: when teams expand into new corridors that fast,
the payout side usually starts creaking before anything else does.

Noah gives you named USD, EUR and GBP accounts plus local payouts in one place, so you
are not stitching together three providers per market.

Worth a quick call next week?

[Your name]
